# Frame Leniency

Gives you a couple of forgiving physics frames so tight parts are easier to hit when recording a level showcase.

- **Hazard grace**: brushing a spike or block wall for 1–2 frames doesn't kill you. Longer contact still does.
- **Input buffering**: very short taps are held a little longer, so a slightly early click still catches the landing or orb.

Both windows are adjustable in the mod settings. Nothing about the level itself changes.

This is a practice/showcase tool, not for verifying levels or leaderboard runs.
